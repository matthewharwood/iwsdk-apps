# Static creature bonuses, development 0.13

This increment implements 41 exact source bodies that continuously increase creature power or toughness. The release contains 1,170 primary card definitions, 20 separate token templates and 46 legal development decks. It preserves all 1,129 previous definitions, every token template and all 40 previous deck revisions exactly after canonical serialization. The full pinned snapshot still has 30,659 unsupported paper candidates.

The release hash is `7f1e1b65f7134dce4add33ded2019720f74b44f0c9c194dbc34e182383914bdf`, using `commander-engine/0.13.0` and `development-match-plan/9`. `bun run commander compile --all-reviewed` reconstructs this release and its decks. The complete source text, source versions and typed programs are retained in [source binding evidence](source-static-bonus-v0.13.json); binding does not itself certify execution.

Research classified all 31,829 paper candidates, retained 68 complete source bodies matching the broad additive pattern, and qualified 41 of them: 30 creatures and 11 non-Aura enchantments. The other 27 bodies remain excluded. Independent review authenticated the 68 raw records, 30 associated rulings, 170 full rule nodes and all 12 archives, including the historical paper printing for Kobold Taskmaster. It independently derived the expected programs and rejected 205 changed-source variants.

The runtime reads live battlefield sources and their current controllers. It selects all creatures, legendary creatures, or creatures of a reviewed subtype; “other” excludes the exact source incarnation. Fixed nonnegative additions apply in layer 7c. Base facts and current characteristics are separate, so selecting a recipient does not recursively calculate its own bonus. Token recipients use their immutable creation template and current characteristics. No durable power/toughness cache is stored.

Sources begin affecting applicable creatures as they enter, and stop when they leave the battlefield. Pending spells and last-known source records provide no live static bonus. Non-Aura enchantments support ordinary casting and resolution, including printed flash timing. State-based actions identify the entire simultaneous lethal batch and its reasons before moving any member. This prevents a dying provider from changing the reason for another death midway through that batch. Fixed additions commute; this is not general timestamp/dependency ordering, type changes, ability removal or power/toughness setting and switching. Duplicate legendary permanents still stop with an explicit unsupported-choice result.

Thirty-eight new source identities execute ordinary legal casting, payment and resolution in the authenticated source suite. Its 46 tests and 992 assertions also exercise shroud/hexproof recipients, actual flash enchantments, static changes to trample assignment, and Arvad the Cursed with Isamaru and Vampire tokens. Inspiring Veteran, Kargan Warleader and Merfolk Mistbinder remain source-bound without legal execution: none of the 39 implemented eligible commanders permits their RW or GU identities. All 117 incompatible admission attempts are checked. Arvad supplies WB identity and closes Call to the Feast's previous legal-execution gap; Arvad boosts legendary creatures, whereas Legion Lieutenant boosts those nonlegendary Vampire tokens.

Six additional profiles use Jasmine Boreal, The Lady of the Mountain, Lady Orca, Tobias Andrion, Sivitri Scarzam and Arvad. Each is a distinct legal 100-card deck with 39 basic lands. Prepared compilation retains only actual deck roots and exact producer-to-token-template edges. A static predicate does not pull every matching card from the catalog into the executable. Both execution factories reject altered source programs and incompatible engine versions even if public hashes are recomputed.

Two selected CLI assignments completed 649 commands for two seats and 1,698 for four. Full-scan, prepared-scan and prepared-indexed modes independently produced identical accepted commands, entitled observations, state boundaries and replay outcomes. Two additional exploratory two-seat assignments completed 376 and 735 commands. The latter was initially invoked with the unsupported `--players 4` argument; its actual manifest correctly says two-seat. The corrected four-seat invocation uses `--mode four-seat`, and both attempts remain retained.

The native durability fixture uses ordinary commands from a legal two-seat setup. Across all three resolvers it saves before Arvad enters at revision 157, after entry at 158, before Repulse at 163, during the owner's replacement choice at 164, and after departure at 165. Isamaru is 4/4 while Arvad remains and 2/2 after either hand or command-zone departure. Seventeen tests with 766 assertions verify reopen, logical import, replay, exact receipts, rollback and tamper rejection. These are stopped ongoing histories, not completed games. The undamaged Isamaru does not prove the separate post-return lethal-damage scenario.

The final browser proof completed 542 commands/543 boundaries for two seats and 1,710/1,711 for four. Each game reloaded the document and opened a new SQLite OPFS worker at every checkpoint. Pending-static, active-static and static-departure snapshots were separately exported, imported into another namespace and retried. Those stages occurred at revisions 135/244/317 and 226/230/1558. The final departure import continued to the same completed history and replay hash. The three stages may involve different provider incarnations; they are not a unified timing proof for one source. Neither browser game encountered commander return, so pending commander-frame import and old choice retry are not claimed here.

Independent artifact review checked actual closed native SQLite histories, saved command/event prefixes, all logical-save checksums, matching browser/native boundaries, all 82 captured source files and the emitted worker. The main checkout reproduces the exact release, deck revisions, captured CLI source and executable bytes, and byte-identical browser worker. Four CLI comparisons plus two selected browser settings account for six full assignments and 5,710 accepted commands. They represent four distinct initial composition/game-seed families. Replays, resolver modes, imports and repeated browser qualification do not increase those counts.

Review also found and fixed `STATIC-HARNESS-01`: a derived-stat difference caused solely by a static bonus incorrectly satisfied the temporary-modifier proof checkpoint. That checkpoint now requires an actual current fixed-target continuous effect. Fourteen focused tests with 55 assertions verify static-only, counters, expiry, changed incarnation and source-departure cases. The original finding and the append-only fix evidence remain archived. A first browser attempt that failed to bind a local server is retained separately.

The complete repository gate passed 905 unit tests with 96,199 assertions, 12 application browser tests, emulated Quest entry/exit and fresh standalone/generated application checks. The fixed 64-game historical-input regression completed and replayed all 64 assignments (89,784 accepted commands); those older deck compositions do not exercise the new static cards. Exact evidence references and remaining gaps are in [development evidence](development-0.13-evidence.json). Full-snapshot coverage, complete original G2 qualification, the production Commander session contract and physical headset testing remain unfinished.
