# Ordinary activated abilities, development 0.18

The current source increment adds 239 complete pinned card bodies with ordinary activated abilities. Its development release contains 1,437 primary definitions, 20 separate token templates and 63 legal 100-card decks. All 1,198 previous definitions, 20 templates and 55 complete deck revisions remain exact. Another 30,392 of the 31,829 eligible source identities remain unsupported. These are source-binding counts, not counts of fully tested cards.

The release is `4efc85ff671b5628c45d617a136f31a5f1d6e4b64067efe39d95525ec61f7e93`, with `commander-engine/0.18.0`. Source review, execution, independent review, persistence and completed-game evidence remain separately identified. The source expansion retains its original pre-execution assurance rather than being rewritten as a complete semantic certificate.

An activation creates a noncard stack ability with an immutable source definition, program, controller and object generation. The owning player selects its required target, activates legal mana sources, and pays the printed fixed mana and/or source tap cost. An absent mana cost remains distinct from an explicit zero cost. Tap costs respect summoning sickness and haste. A paid ability can outlive its source, while a modifier referring to that source cannot accidentally affect a new incarnation after a zone change. Targets are checked again on resolution.

The admitted bodies cover 118 self power/toughness abilities, 57 self keyword abilities, 22 target-creature power/toughness abilities, 22 tap-target-creature abilities, 11 draw abilities and nine life-gain abilities. These programs do not yet cover variable costs, sacrifice costs, mana output or token production. Their typed structures extend the same command, replay and storage boundary used by spells and triggered abilities.

Independent review reproduced an actual priority defect: announcing and cancelling an unpaid activation erased earlier priority passes. The fix preserves those passes through reversible announcement; a real mana action or completed payment still resets them. Thirty independent tests with 219 assertions passed across all three resolver modes. Constructed hands, control changes and empty libraries in that review are explicitly distinguished from ordinary deals.

The authored source suite executes 234 of the 239 newly bound identities in three resolver configurations, plus its inventory assertion: 703 tests. Five identities lack an appropriate commander among the 63 retained deck profiles: Angelfire Crusader, Kranioceros, Leaping Master, Torch Drake and Towering Thunderfist. Their source bindings do not imply that those five cards executed in this release's legal-deck suite. The broader runtime capture passed 1,605 tests and retained one default-timeout failure; that exact case passed unchanged in a separately recorded rerun. Native durability passed 12 tests with 345 assertions.

The native durability history uses an ordinary legal deal and stops at revision 161. Its six checkpoints cover target selection, payment, mana activation, paid stack ability, resolved effect and source departure. Full-scan, prepared-scan and prepared-indexed each reopen, import, replay and retry these boundaries. The seven-definition fixture has no token templates; its full and prepared registries both need all seven definitions, so this fixture proves no registry-size reduction.

Real Chromium workers replay those stopped histories using SQLite WASM OPFS. All 18 reloads, 18 separate imports and 36 exact receipt retries passed. Fifteen imports advance to the retained final prefix; three already contain it. These histories remain ongoing and add no completed games.

The selected complete CLI games finish in 639 and 2,271 commands. All three resolver modes agree on decisions, observations, events, state boundaries and replay. The histories contain 24 and 71 paid, resolved activations across 15 distinct source identities in their union. Each counted occurrence joins its accepted announcement, immutable source program, payment and later ability resolution.

The selected complete browser games finish in 657 and 2,271 commands, with 24 and 68 paid, resolved activations. Every native/browser state boundary agrees, including completion after reload and logical import. Different seat identities can change heuristic tie-breaking; the browser totals are read from their own tapes. Their full-game reload checkpoints cover ordinary spell payment. Pending activation checkpoints are proved by the separate stopped histories above.

A proof helper originally recognized only spell-created continuous effects. It now joins both spell and activated-ability resolution to the correct retained source, payment and event order. Twenty-three tests with 133 assertions validate the helper, including negative mutations. The initial failed browser attempts are retained separately from the corrected passing runs.

The public `compile --all-reviewed` CLI reproduces the complete 1,437-definition release and all 63 deck revisions. Main-workspace executable verification matches the actual CLI bytes and browser worker bytes used by the final game proofs. The fixed regression completed and replayed all 64 exact older-deck assignments, covering 89,784 accepted commands. A separate audit verified the raw SQLite outcomes, command identities, receipt/event hashes and original deck/seed assignments. These old decks contain no ordinary activations. The full main repository gate passed: 2,307 tests and 188,252 assertions, production build, 12 browser tests, emulated Quest smoke, and standalone workspace/generated-application validation. The generated workspace reran the same 2,307-test corpus; those repeats do not multiply semantic coverage.

The next source increment revealed a historical state-based-action defect: a surviving creature's deathtouch flag can remain set after the relevant check. That repair belongs to the next engine ABI and is not silently inserted into these frozen replay tapes. Whole-card obligation closure, full G2, broader costs/triggers/layers, the full-snapshot portable handoff, production Commander browser/XR sessions and physical Quest verification remain unfinished.
