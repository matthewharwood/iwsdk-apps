# Static keyword grants — engine 0.20

This frozen prototype adds 55 complete source-bound permanents whose static abilities grant creature keywords. The release has 1,638 primary definitions, 20 auxiliary token templates and 74 legal development decks. All 1,583 previous definitions, all token templates and all 69 previous deck revisions remain exact. The pinned snapshot still has 30,191 unsupported eligible identities. The composed 0.19 and 0.20 source is now promoted to the main workspace, and its full repository gate passed.

The mechanism evaluates live battlefield providers in layer 6. Its finite selectors combine source-controller or any-controller scope with creature type, optional additional type, subtype, color and source exclusion. Grants follow the provider's current incarnation and current applicable controller. Leaving the battlefield ends the grant; returning creates a new incarnation. Existing printed and temporary keywords combine with these grants. This does not implement arbitrary type/control changes, ability removal, timestamps or dependencies across all continuous-effect layers.

The source set includes 41 own-controller providers and 14 providers that apply across controllers. The source review preserves the exact word “creatures”; Crystalline Sliver's broader subtype-only wording remains an unsupported contrast. Five new deck profiles make all 55 grant sources available in legal decks. Sliver Hivelord also makes 27 prior color-identity gaps legally deckable; availability alone does not claim those older cards have been executed or fully qualified.

The independent source review preceded code inspection. A separate compiled-definition join checked all 55 whole-source meanings and rejected 1,297 source mutations plus 440 definition mutations. It found and corrected a missing shadow rule reference for Shadow Sliver. The earlier release and failed evidence remain archived. The author source/compiler suite passed 535 tests and 111,814 assertions. Public CLI compilation independently reproduced the exact 1,638-definition release and 74 deck revisions.

The combined core qualification passed 2,430 tests and 30,448 assertions with 322 captured inputs unchanged. It includes actual paid casts of all 55 authenticated grant providers under three factory-admitted resolver configurations: 165 source cases and 3,135 assertions. Those source fixtures use 77 full definitions or seven/eight prepared definitions and legal 100-card decks, with constructed selected hands, lands and opposing witnesses. Separate constructed combat and consequence tests isolate interactions. They are not complete games.

Eight independently authored constructed scenario families passed under three in-memory selector modes: 24 tests and 222 assertions. This review distinguishes its synthetic prepared-mode fixture from actual prepared-artifact admission. It checks selector combinations, source departure/recast, changed blocking restrictions and deathtouch consequences. Its 28-scenario expectation join retains partial and deferred coverage explicitly.

The work exposed a rules defect in earlier executors: a deathtouch damage flag persisted past a completed state-based check. Engine 0.20 snapshots current death eligibility, then clears these “since the last check” flags without clearing ordinary marked damage. A creature that survived nonlethal deathtouch damage while indestructible does not die later merely because indestructible is lost; newly dealt deathtouch still counts. The corrected pre-fix reproduction failed six cases across three modes. The failed inputs, repair, independent verification and old executors are retained. Historical tapes keep their actual ABI identity.

Real CLI games completed in 490 and 2,448 accepted commands, with nine and nineteen paid grant-provider resolutions. All three resolver executions and replays matched. Root audit replayed the stored full-scan histories and independently evaluated every live creature's keyword set against direct source predicates, covering 18,119 creature boundaries. The histories contain 22 distinct applicable provider definitions. A provider being applicable is not necessarily its unique causal contribution when another source grants the same keyword.

Real browser SQLite OPFS games completed in 490 and 2,089 commands, with nine and sixteen new paid provider resolutions. The worker matched the native engine at every boundary. Root audit joined actual database commands, requests, receipts, events, source announcements, payments and permanent incarnations.

The storage qualification uses one ordinary legal 100-card deck/seed family with a nine-definition source fixture. Sliver Hivelord resolves, then Metallic Sliver enters. Sorin's Thirst marks two damage on the 1/1 recipient; the creature remains on the battlefield while Hivelord grants indestructible. Repulse targets Hivelord and pauses for its owner's command-zone-or-hand choice. Both branches move Hivelord, complete Repulse's draw, then let the damaged recipient die during state-based actions. The replay does not start from a constructed battlefield.
