# Mandatory entry observers, development 0.14

Development 0.14 implements 20 exact card bodies that observe permanents entering the battlefield, then mandatorily gain life or draw. It has 1,190 primary definitions, 20 separate token templates and 49 legal development decks. Every prior definition, token template and all 46 earlier deck revisions remain exact after canonical serialization. The pinned snapshot still has 30,639 unsupported paper candidates. The native and browser histories are independently reviewed; this document is not a full-snapshot coverage claim.

The release is `19b625376b02555521b2f0cea2a33097299bac813e65b682725982e61bfd948d`, with `commander-engine/0.14.0` and `development-match-plan/10`. The main `compile --all-reviewed` command reproduces the same release and all 49 decks. Prepared compilation retains actual deck roots and exact token-template edges. An observer of creatures or artifacts does not acquire a dependency on every such card in the catalog.

The finite source investigation classified all 31,829 paper candidates and retained 179 related source records, 578 rulings and 250 full Comprehensive Rules nodes. Semantic review examined 37 complete bodies: 21 mandatory bodies, nine optional bodies and seven bodies with additional unsupported conditions. Twenty mandatory bodies were selected. Tanglespan Lookout is deferred because its Aura-entry subject has no admitted Aura producer; it is not treated as executed through another enchantment. Independent review re-authenticated the records and archives, compared all 20 resulting programs against separately authored expectations, and rejected 421 changed-definition/source variants.

The supported subjects are a Creature, Land, Artifact or Enchantment permanent, or a permanent with subtype Beast. Printed controller and “other” restrictions remain exact. Woodland Liege's Beast condition does not imply Creature: its constructed boundary test includes a noncreature Kindred Beast, while its actual source execution uses Primal Huntbeast. Eidolon of Blossoms, Kor Celebrant and Bogwater Lumaret have an explicit self alternative; it is an identity union that matches once, independent of the other branch's descriptive filter.

The core first commits the complete entry batch, then matches every existing and newly entered battlefield observer against every entrant. Legal land plays, permanent spell resolutions and token creation share that entry hook. Each occurrence captures its source and controller, the entering subject incarnation and source version, selector facts, program and event coordinates. Source and subject are distinct historical objects. Neither is selected again when the ability resolves. The core preserves this context when the source leaves, when a token subject ceases, and when an original owner departs while the captured controller survives.

State-based actions precede ordinary trigger placement. The captured controller chooses an exact permutation of their waiting abilities in APNAP order. Each complete fixed sequence resolves before another priority or state-based-action checkpoint. Tatyova gains one life and then draws one card. Optional effects, intervening-if clauses, trigger-on-trigger second-wave processing, general control/type changes and entry replacements are not part of this increment.

All 20 source bodies execute matching entry effects after ordinary casting and payment in authenticated legal 100-card scenarios. Their selected hands and existing lands are explicit constructed preconditions, not claimed random opening deals. The suite also executes nine previously bound GU cards under newly admitted Tatyova, including Jungle Barrier's self-entry draw and Merfolk Mistbinder's bonus to Tatyova. This closes Mistbinder's prior commander-compatibility gap. Inspiring Veteran and Kargan Warleader still lack a compatible implemented RW commander.

Source interactions include a legal Forest play observed by Tatyova and Jaddi Offshoot, two Queen's Commission tokens producing four distinct Warden/Healer occurrences, Soul Warden retaining its effect after Repulse, and a countered Eidolon producing no entry trigger. Sixty-five source/core/contract tests passed with 848 assertions; the broader contracts, engine and simulation run passed 631 tests with 8,978 assertions. Independent review added six source-owner/token-subject departure cases across all three resolver modes. Its combined 35-test run passed 244 assertions. These source and constructed tests remain separate from complete games.
