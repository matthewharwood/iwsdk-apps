# Damage replacement and prevention, development 0.17

This increment executes the complete pinned bodies of Furnace of Rath, Dictate of the Twin Gods, Benevolent Unicorn, Urza's Armor and Excruciator. The development release contains 1,198 primary definitions, 20 separate token templates and 55 legal decks. All 1,193 previous definitions, 20 templates and 53 deck revisions remain exact. Another 30,631 of the 31,829 eligible identities remain unsupported.

The release is `77857dc934ac410326f5a1136bca66757c33ae7ae74326c9d3f0f354432810de`, using `commander-engine/0.17.0`. The [machine-readable evidence](development-0.17-evidence.json) joins source review, authored assertions, independent review, native durability, completed games and browser execution. The [source expansion](source-damage-replacement-v0.17.json) retains its original source-binding assurance; its compilation count is separate from later execution evidence.

Damage is proposed before it changes life or marked damage. The affected player, or affected permanent's current controller, chooses between applicable ordinary replacement and prevention effects. Each choice records the exact event version, occurrence, source provider and effect. The engine applies an effect at most once to that occurrence, recomputes applicability, and requests another owned choice when necessary. Damage commits after those choices finish. The enclosing spell continues from its saved instruction position, so Sorin's Thirst gains life exactly once after its damage completes.

The five source bodies exercise doubling, subtraction restricted to spell damage, prevention for a provider's controller, and damage that cannot be prevented. Flash remains part of Dictate's complete body. Source departure, controller ownership, repeated-choice rejection and damage arithmetic are covered by separate source and constructed scenarios. These programs do not implement general redirection, consumable shields, entry/copy replacement precedence or all event classes.

Compiler and consumer captures passed 164 tests with 56,023 assertions and 18 tests with 967 assertions. These totals overlap other checks and are not added into a unique test count. Independent review retained and corrected six proof-predicate false positives; actual coordinator replay remains a separate requirement.
